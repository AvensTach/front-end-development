Goal of the Web Portal

Our main goal is to provide accessible and high-quality education in web development for everyone.

Tasks to achieve the goal:

  1. PROVIDE USERS WITH A SOLID THEORETICAL FOUNDATION IN JAVASCRIPT.
  2. OFFER PRACTICAL TASKS TO CONSOLIDATE THE LEARNED MATERIAL.
  3. CREATE A PLATFORM FOR COMMUNICATION AND FEEDBACK AMONG STUDENTS.

In our course you will find interesting lessons, where each topic is explained in a very detailed manner.
