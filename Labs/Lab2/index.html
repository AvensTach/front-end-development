Goal of the Web Portal

Our main goal is to provide accessible and high-quality education in web development for everyone. We believe that technology should be open to all, regardless of their background. In this detailed course, we cover everything from basic syntax to advanced topics like asynchronous programming, DOM manipulation, and modern frameworks. Our community is growing every day, and we are excited to have you on board. Keep learning, keep coding, and build amazing things!

Tasks to achieve the goal:

  1. Provide users with a solid theoretical foundation in JavaScript.
  2. Offer practical tasks to consolidate the learned material.
  3. Create a platform for communication.

General Information about the Language

JavaScript is a high-level programming language that is one of the core technologies of the World Wide Web.
